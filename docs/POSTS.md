# Reddit post (r/op1)

**Title:** BLOODMOON: a gothic organ + haunted bell synth engine plugin for the original OP-1 (op1r, free, GPL)

**Body:**

BLOODMOON is a new synth engine for the original OP-1. It's a dark organ with a dissonant bell struck on top, made for gothic ambient, witch house and ruined cathedrals. It's an op1r plugin file (`.op1p`) that you install with the Hundekraft OP-1 Workshop, and it shows up in T1 next to the other engines.

🎥 30-second demo: [link]

**The four knobs**
- **Blood** (blue): harmonic richness, from hollow and thin to thick and dark
- **Eclipse** (green): the bell strike, from none to loud, long and dissonant
- **Haunting** (white): vibrato that fades in after the attack, plus slow pitch drift like old pipes
- **Moonrise** (orange): vibrato speed, from about 0.15 Hz to 9 Hz

**The screen** is a cathedral under a blood moon. Each knob moves its own part of it: the moon rises, the rose window turns, the bells swing and the stars flicker. Each knob's name and value are shown in blackletter on both sides of the screen.

**8 presets** are included: cathedral, funeral bell, crypt drone, witch choir, hollow saint, moon tremor, sunken pipes and blood rite.

**Install**
1. You need an original OP-1 (not the Field) on firmware R246, plus its original `op1_246.op1` file.
2. Back up your tapes and presets.
3. In the [Hundekraft OP-1 Workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/index.html), add `bloodmoon.op1p` under "Your plugins", build, and install.
4. Presets: in disk mode, copy the `bloodmoon` folder into `synth`.

**Status:** tested on my OP-1 and working. The volume is set so it doesn't crackle the built-in speaker. The presets are new, so tell me if one doesn't load. Recovery works as usual: if anything goes wrong, hold COM while switching on and install a build without it.

Download (plugin file, presets, full source): [link]

It's free and GPL-3.0 (with the op1r OP-1 firmware permission), and the full source and build kit are included. Thanks to pcppcp and contributors for op1r, Hundekraft for the Workshop, and nvmsocool's Brassophone for showing the way. The lettering is Pirata One (SIL OFL).

Feedback and bug reports are very welcome. Please include your firmware version and what you heard or saw.
